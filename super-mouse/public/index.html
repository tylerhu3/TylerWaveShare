How it connects

Plug the badge into this computer with USB, open Super Mouse on the badge, then click Connect Badge (USB) and pick the USB JTAG/serial debug unit port. The badge sends everything to its Bluetooth host (tap the target pill on the badge to switch hosts).

Needs Chrome or Edge (WebSerial). Close idf.py monitor first: only one app can hold the port.
